The business is owned by the Diocese of Karonga and supported by Missio USA. The farm is managed and developed by trusted experts with deep knowledge of the local agricultural landscape.

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Plenty of water available from Katumbuka river
  • Secure title to the land (99-year lease)
  • Large proportion of blocks already terraced
  • Established macadamia and coffee trees already on farm
  • Natural shade tree coverage for coffee farming
  • Nearness to other commercial producers for knowledge transfer
  • Farm manager and staff reside on the farm

Weaknesses

  • Some areas of the farm not yet cleared for farming
  • Sloppy terrain increases maintenance costs
  • Insufficient nursery capacity for seedling demand
  • No electrical power on the farm
  • Significant skills gap in financial management
  • Insufficient financial resources for expansion

Opportunities

  • Attractive global prices for coffee
  • Elaborate transport system for domestic and export markets
  • Tropical climate ideal for macadamia and coffee
  • Stable government in Malawi
  • Growing demand for specialty African coffee
  • Malawi is 4th largest macadamia exporter worldwide

Threats

  • Global fluctuations of coffee and macadamia prices
  • Soaring global inflation increasing production costs
  • Climate change affecting farming operations
  • Reported reduction in annual rainfall
  • Lack of quality extension services
  • Currency devaluation risks

Risk Mitigation Strategies

Environmental Risk

56 hectares of natural woodlands remain untouched to protect water sources. Existing trees provide shade for coffee plantations and enable Rainforest Alliance certification.

Low Yields

Installation of irrigation system ensures constant water supply. High level agronomic care given to planted crops. Drip irrigation planned for both coffee and macadamia.

Pests and Diseases

Good farm management through variety choice, shade management, selective pesticide use, and plant nutrition. Constant monitoring and training of field workers.

Theft and Damage

Management ensures mature nuts are collected immediately when they fall to the ground. Perimeter fence planned for security.
