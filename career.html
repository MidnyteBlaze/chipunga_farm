Join Our Team

Chipunga Farms Limited is committed to creating meaningful employment opportunities in Nkhata Bay District. We currently employ 27 permanent staff and engage casual workers as needed. With our expansion plans, we will create 60 permanent and 100 temporary positions.

We are looking for dedicated, hardworking individuals who share our commitment to sustainable agriculture and community development.

Working Hours

Standard Working Hours

Hours: 8 hours per day (excluding lunch break)

Working Week: 40 hours, Monday to Friday

Official Hours: 7:30am to 5:00pm (includes 1.5 hours lunch break)

Security Guards: Maximum 48 hours per week

Overtime Policy

No employee shall render overtime work without prior approval from their immediate supervisor.

Staff required to work additional hours, particularly on weekends and public holidays, may be granted compensatory time off or additional annual leave at the Company's discretion.

Leave Policies

Leave Type Entitlement Additional Information
Annual Leave 21 days per year Applications need to be lodged one week in advance. Annual leave grant depending on employee's rank as determined by the Board.
Sick Leave As per Malawi Employment Act Medical certificate may be required at management's discretion.
Compassionate Leave 2 days paid per occasion For death or serious illness of a family member. Additional unpaid leave may be granted.
Maternity Leave 90 calendar days with full pay Once every 3 years. Notice required 3 months before expected date. Breastfeeding: 2 hours per day for 6 months after delivery.
Paternity Leave 5 working days Within 7 days after delivery. Once every 3 years. Birth report required.
Study Leave Long term study exceeding 12 months Only permanent employees with minimum 3 consecutive years of service are eligible.
Unpaid Leave Maximum 3 months At management discretion for pressing domestic or personal problems.

Employee Benefits

Remuneration

  • Competitive salaries based on Company salary scale
  • Salaries payable on 25th of every month
  • Statutory deductions indicated on monthly pay slip
  • Salary advance available for emergencies (up to 50% of monthly salary)

Health & Safety

  • Safe work environment with protective equipment provided
  • Workers' compensation benefits if injured at work
  • First aiders available on site
  • Clean toilets, drinking water, and hygienic eating areas

Work Environment

  • Equal opportunity employer
  • Workplace free from discrimination and harassment
  • Flexible working arrangements for parental and carer responsibilities
  • Performance-based salary increases

Probation Period

6 months probation for all new employees. 3 months for those who have been with the Company as interns or volunteers. Ongoing permanent employment is given only when the employee satisfactorily completes their probation.

Code of Conduct

Expected Conduct

  • Observe all policies and procedures
  • Treat colleagues with courtesy and respect
  • Treat customers and clients professionally at all times
  • Work safely at all times
  • Maintain clean, neat, and professional dress
  • Use internet for business purposes (limited private use permitted)

Prohibited Conduct

  • Smoking on Company property
  • Attending work under influence of alcohol or drugs (zero tolerance)
  • Theft or misuse of Company assets
  • Discrimination, sexual harassment, or bullying
  • Falsification of records
  • Disclosure of confidential information

Why Work With Us?

Join a growing agribusiness that values its employees, offers competitive benefits, and contributes to community development in Northern Malawi. Be part of a team that is building a sustainable future for coffee and macadamia production.
