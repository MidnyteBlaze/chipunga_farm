About Chipunga Farm

Chipunga Farm is a premium coffee and macadamia farm in Nkhata Bay, Northern Malawi. Set in high-altitude terrain with fertile soils, cool temperatures, and reliable rainfall, we practise sustainable farming to produce exceptional crops.

CHIPUNGAFARMSLTD

Chipunga Farms are located in Nkhata Bay, in the cool highlands of Viphya Plateau in Northern Malawi. Nestled near the natural woodlands of Kaningina Forest, the farm benefits from rich soils, steady rainfall, high altitude and temperatures ideal for producing quality coffee and macadamia.
